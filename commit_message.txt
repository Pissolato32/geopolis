Perf(map): cache projected unit coordinates in WorldMap

## 💡 What
Cached the projected unit coordinates instead of re-calculating them inside `hitUnit` on every single mousemove event.
Introduced `projectedUnits` via `useMemo` that depends on `units`, `size.w`, and `size.h`.

## 🎯 Why
Repeatedly running the d3 geographic projection mapping on every unit for every frame/mouse movement was an unnecessary overhead. The coordinates only need to be recomputed when the physical window size changes (which alters the projection) or when the list/positions of units change.

## 📊 Measured Improvement
Before the optimization, the benchmark (`src/hitUnit.benchmark.test.ts`) running 1,000 mouse events against 1,000 units resulted in:
*   **Baseline time:** ~146.33ms
*   **Baseline projection calls:** ~721,741
*   **Optimized time:** ~22.66ms
*   **Optimized projection calls:** 1000 (just the initial cache phase)

This effectively converts the `hitUnit` O(m * u) projection scaling to O(u), resulting in a ~84% performance improvement for hit detection logic while preventing thousands of wasted projection calculations.

## ⚙️ Correctness and Limitations
*   Cache invalidation correctly hooks into `units` and `size` (which are the logical drivers of the `geoEqualEarth().fitSize` projection in `WorldMap.tsx`).
*   Existing CI and test suites pass cleanly.
*   Added benchmark tests specifically testing the caching and radius hitting behavior.
